## B01 registration invitation QR integration

- Added an independent B01 registration-invitation flow. A successful registration
  dispatches only its database `regSno`; eHIS then validates the antiforgery token,
  B01 menu access, same-day eligible state, and exact `registered_user_id` ownership
  before requesting a scoped backend invitation. The existing doctor/substitute
  invitation authorization and its current-encounter state policy remain unchanged.
- The registration screen renders the returned one-time patient URL with the local
  Syncfusion QR component. Invitation errors are shown separately with retry support,
  so a 403, 404, or upstream 502 cannot turn a completed registration into a failed
