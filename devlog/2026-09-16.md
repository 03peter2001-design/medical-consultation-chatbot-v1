# 2026-09-16

## MedGemma 27B image-reading ablation v0.2.0

- Reframed the study around image-specific evidence instead of relying on the
  original NEJM question, which often includes a formal interpretation of the image.
- Added four paired conditions for every case: patient-actor proxy context only,
  matched image, deterministic subtype-near mismatched image, and image only. The
  proxy context limitation is recorded explicitly because it was not manually
  redacted by clinicians.
- Completed 480/480 scorable responses across 120 cases. Accuracy was 65.0% for
  proxy context only, 62.5% with the matched image, 66.7% with a mismatched image,
  and 25.8% for image only. Matched images produced 2 helpful and 5 harmful flips
  versus no image (`p=0.453`) and 3 helpful and 8 harmful flips versus wrong images
  (`p=0.227`). Image-only performance was not significantly above 20% five-choice
  chance (`p=0.072`).
- The 90 direct-diagnosis cases showed the same pattern: 70.0%, 66.7%, 67.8%, and
  23.3%, respectively. X-ray was the only adequately sized subtype with a positive
  matched-image gain (+8.3 points), but matched and mismatched X-rays had identical
  accuracy, so visual specificity was not established.
- Added a dedicated visual analyzer, deterministic wrong-image selection, provenance
  fields, regression tests, and a tracked reference report. The decision is to run a
  specialist-model proof of concept before considering integration; vLLM is treated
  as a serving/runtime option rather than an image-capability intervention.
- No new model weights, inference servers, or dependencies were installed. The
  experiment remains a research screen and does not establish clinical safety.

## MedGemma 27B AgentClinic-NEJM subtype evaluation v0.1.0

- Added an isolated `AgentClinic/medgemma_eval/` research harness; the legacy
  AgentClinic runner and other project services are unchanged.
- The harness validates all 120 extended NEJM cases, preserves their 13 multi-label
  subtypes, and distinguishes 90 direct-diagnosis questions from 30 other clinical
  question intents.
- Paired text-only and text-plus-image runs use deterministic per-case option order,
  Ollama structured output, exact A–E scoring, bounded retries, immediate JSONL
  persistence, and resumable keys. Results capture model/data/prompt/image provenance.
- Analysis keeps inference and parse failures in the primary denominator and reports
  per-subtype accuracy with Wilson intervals plus image-helpful and image-harmful
  flips. Subtypes below 10 paired cases are marked descriptive only.
- Added image caching with content validation and SHA-256 manifests, an all-subtype
  seven-case smoke cohort, and deterministic unit coverage for dataset invariants,
  task classification, option shuffling, parsing, multi-label aggregation, failure
  denominators, paired flips, and confidence intervals.
- The tool and reports state that public-case contamination is possible and that the
  benchmark is not clinical validation or a basis for patient care.
- Completed the full 120-case paired reference run with 240/240 scorable responses.
  Text-only accuracy was 72.5% and text-plus-image accuracy was 70.8%; the 90-case
  direct-diagnosis subset measured 76.7% and 73.3%, respectively. The paired image
  effects were not statistically persuasive (all-case exact McNemar p=0.727;
  diagnosis-only p=0.375). A tracked reference report records subtype results,
  provenance, runtime characteristics, and interpretation limits.
