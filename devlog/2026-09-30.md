## Chief-complaint extraction truncation fix (Backend v0.16.1)

- An ER-Reason pilot showed about half of all sessions handing off at the first
  or second turn. The chief-complaint extractor's 1,200-token budget truncated the
  JSON for realistic multi-symptom complaints. The engine then failed closed as
  designed.
- The budget is now the named constant `CHIEF_EXTRACTION_MAX_TOKENS=4096`.
- `LLMClient` retries once with a larger limit on any Gemini `MAX_TOKENS` response,
  not only an empty one. Safety rules, the extraction prompt, the schema, and
  validation are unchanged.
- A second `MAX_TOKENS` response now fails closed even when non-empty, so a
  partial physician report cannot be treated as complete.
- Synthetic complaints: `gemini-3.6-flash` went from 0/3 to 7/7 valid extractions,
  and `gemini-2.5-flash` from 2/4 long complaints to 7/7.
- The full backend suite ran 510 tests. The same 3 failures and 2 errors occur
  with or without this change; they are pre-existing RAG/UCC issues.

## ER-Reason MedKGI pilot (LLM experiments v0.9.0, research only)

- A new ignored harness `llm_experiments.er_reason` drives the real backend
  `AMIEEngine` with a Gemini patient simulator. The simulator only sees the ED
  note text before the first exam/assessment marker.
- Data: credentialed PhysioNet ER-Reason v1.0.0. The dataset stays in ignored
  `benchmarks/` and results stay in ignored `local/`. No note text is recorded
  here.
- Labels: ED diagnoses were mapped to the 33 provisional profiles. Gemini drafted
  the mapping and Claude reviewed it (`ai_reviewed`, not clinician-reviewed; 2
  corrections). This gave 92 eligible cases, one per patient: abdomen 67, chest
  18, headache 7.
- Run 2 used the fixed backend and production `gemini-3.6-flash` extraction. Both
  arms used the same 92 cases and 0 cases errored.

  | Metric | MedKGI profile_experiment | unit_vote |
  | --- | --- | --- |
  | Top-1 | 38/92 | 36/92 |
  | Top-5 | 65/92 (70.7%, CI 60.7–79.0%) | 62/92 (67.4%) |
  | Must-not-miss safe (top-5 or urgent escalation) | 10/20 | 15/20 |
  | Handoffs | 8 | 11 |
  | "Don't know" answers | ~37% | ~37% |

- None of the paired differences are significant (exact McNemar p 0.13–0.82).
  MedKGI's lower must-not-miss rate is the main safety signal to follow up; for
  example, pulmonary embolism was in the top 5 in 1/4 MedKGI cases versus 4/4
  unit_vote cases.
- 30–31 cases per arm are flagged because the gold disease name appears in the
  simulator view or answers.
- Limitations: the likelihoods are assumed (0.8/0.2/0.5), labels are ED
  diagnoses, routing is oracle, the samples per profile are small, the data comes
  from a single English-language center, and the Chinese answers are
  model-translated. This is not clinical validation.
- The remaining handoffs are extractor schema `ValidationError`s on free-text
  answers. They are not addressed in this change.

## ER-Reason third arm: default fixed questionnaire + RAG + Gemini report (LLM experiments v0.10.0)

- The comparison adds `questionnaire_rag`, which reproduces production
  `INTERVIEW_ENGINE=questionnaire`. It asks the full questionnaire in source
  order, then uses RAG knowledge base A (v2 index) and the report prompt
  `fixed-questionnaire-complete-drug-history-v9` on `gemini-3.6-flash`.
- Only the differential (at most 3) and must-not-miss (at most 5) sections run.
  Their prompt inputs are identical to a full report.
- The report's free-text names are mapped to profile IDs by the same prompt
  that labelled the gold standard; the mapper sees no case data.
- All arms share the same 92 cases, the same patient AI, and oracle routing. 0
  cases errored.

  | Metric | questionnaire_rag | unit_vote (amie) | medkgi |
  | --- | --- | --- | --- |
  | Top-1 | 58/92 (63%) | 36/92 (39%) | 38/92 (41%) |
  | Top-3 | 72/92 (78%) | 48/92 (52%) | 49/92 (53%) |
  | Must-not-miss safe | 16/20 | 15/20 | 10/20 |
  | Mean turns | 16.5 | 8.3 | 8.4 |

- Paired exact McNemar: questionnaire_rag beats both engines on top-1 and top-3
  (p < 0.001). The two engines do not differ (p ≥ 0.8). Among the 55 cases
  without a leak flag in any arm, top-3 is 38, 17, and 27 respectively.
- Caveats:
  - The report arm asks about twice as many questions and may name any disease.
  - Its hits are judged by model mapping, not exact IDs.
  - The patient simulator, report, and mapper all use `gemini-3.6-flash`
    (shared-model bias).
- Production routing gap: `local_complaint_route` returned no route for 62/92
  simulated chief complaints. It matches canonical terms (腹痛, 胸痛, 頭痛) but
  misses colloquial phrasing such as 肚子很痛, 頭很痛, and 胸口很悶, which would
  skip the disease questionnaire in production. The oracle-routed score above
  therefore overstates the default path's real-world performance. No routing
  change was made.

## ER-Reason equal-condition replay (LLM experiments v0.11.0)

- Every method read the same 92 full fixed-questionnaire transcripts (from the
  questionnaire_rag run; no new patient simulation).
- AMIE unit vote and MedKGI scored one shared set of engine-extracted facts. The
  median was 8 fact codes per case, and 7 cases had at least one extraction
  failure.
- Gemini (`gemini-3.6-flash` through the backend `LLMClient`, as in production)
  ranked at most 5 IDs from the same closed profile list, with and without RAG
  knowledge base A.
- All methods were scored by exact profile ID. Safety was recorded without
  stopping the replay; it would have escalated 11 cases, and this escalation is
  not counted as a hit for any method.

  | Method | Top-1 | Top-3 | Top-5 | Must-not-miss in top 5 |
  | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
  | Gemini closed + RAG | 60/92 | 77/92 (84%) | 82/92 | 18/20 |
  | Gemini closed, no RAG | 59/92 | 76/92 (83%) | 80/92 | 18/20 |
  | AMIE unit vote | 43/92 | 57/92 (62%) | 73/92 | 15/20 |
  | MedKGI profile_experiment | 40/92 | 54/92 (59%) | 70/92 | 11/20 |
  | Reference: production open report (model-mapped) | 58/92 | 72/92 | — | — |

- Paired exact McNemar tests:
  - Both Gemini arms beat both engines on top-1 and top-3 (p ≤ 0.0015).
  - RAG changes nothing (1 versus 2 discordant cases).
  - MedKGI and AMIE do not differ (p ≥ 0.5).
  - The closed-list Gemini arms match the production open report (p ≥ 0.13).
- On the 56 cases without a leak flag, top-3 is 75%, 73%, 46%, and 45%.
- Interpretation:
  - With equal information, the engines improve over the live arms (top-3 went
    from 52–53% to 59–62%). Question count explained part of the earlier gap,
    but most of the gap remains.
  - The remaining gap sits in the engines' fact extraction plus profile-scoring
    rules, not in RAG.
  - One inequality remains: the patient simulator and the Gemini arms share
    `gemini-3.6-flash`.
